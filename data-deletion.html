Delete from Weatherly

  1. Open AccountSign in to Weatherly and open the Account tab.
  2. Choose Delete accountRead the permanent-deletion explanation. Choose “Keep my account” if you do not wish to proceed.
  3. Verify and deleteSelect a Google or Facebook account already linked to your Weatherly account and complete provider verification.
  4. Wait for confirmationThe app removes your Weatherly profile and sign-in record, then returns to the welcome screen. If a connection problem interrupts deletion, retry the pending-deletion flow.

Account deletion is permanent. Verifying a different provider account will not authorize deletion of your Weatherly account.

Request deletion by email

You can request deletion even if you cannot sign in, no longer have the app installed or prefer to contact us directly.

Email nexoraicc2026@gmail.com with the subject Weatherly account deletion. Include your Weatherly username and whether you used Google or Facebook. Where possible, send the request from the email address associated with the linked provider account.

Request deletion by email

This opens your email application. Review and send the message to submit your request. If no email application opens, copy the address above into your email service.

We may ask you to demonstrate control of the linked account before acting. Never send your password, login verification codes or access tokens.

What is removed

  • Your Weatherly Firebase Authentication account and its Weatherly provider links.
  • Your custom profile, including first name and username.

The app also attempts to clear the cached location on the device used for deletion. To remove remaining local preferences or caches, use Android Settings → Apps → Weatherly → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Your Google/Facebook account itself, emails in your own mailbox and data independently held by service providers are not deleted by removing Weatherly.

SOS data on your phone

In the SOS tab, choose Clear SOS data & stop receiving to remove saved contacts, message, local alerts and the installation signing key. Sign-out or completed in-app account deletion also invokes SOS cleanup on that phone. If cleanup is interrupted, use Android app-storage controls to remove remaining local data.

Deleting your cloud account by email cannot remotely erase an offline phone. Messages already sent through a carrier or received by nearby devices cannot be recalled; their recipients may retain copies.

Timing and possible retention

In-app deletion runs while you are connected. Wait for its completion message. If deletion is interrupted, the app keeps a local pending indicator so you can verify and retry.

For email requests, our target is within 30 days after ownership is verified. We will confirm completion, or explain any extra information or time needed.

Provider security logs/backups may remain under the providers’ own policies. If a legal or security requirement means we must retain particular information, we will explain the relevant categories and reason when applicable. We retain request correspondence only as needed to handle and document the request.

Common questions

Does uninstalling Weatherly delete my account?

No. Uninstalling removes app files from that device but does not submit a cloud-account deletion request. Use the app’s deletion flow or email us.

Does removing Google/Facebook access delete Weatherly?

No. Revoking provider access and deleting your Weatherly account are separate actions.

What if I cannot verify in the app?

Email nexoraicc2026@gmail.com. Explain which provider you used and the problem you encountered. We will explain how to verify ownership without asking for your password or login tokens.

Can I delete data without accessing the app?

Yes. The email route on this page is available without installing or signing in to Weatherly. State whether you are requesting account deletion, a correction or another privacy action.
