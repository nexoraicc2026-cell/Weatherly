Information we handle

Account and provider information

Google or Facebook verifies your provider account. Firebase Authentication processes the linked provider identity, which may include account identifiers, email address, display name and profile-image information supplied by that provider.

Weatherly’s separate profile database stores first name and username under your Firebase account identifier. Registration also asks for a last name, but that input is not stored in this custom profile. Provider-managed records may independently contain a full name.

Passwords and sessions

Your Weatherly password is sent over HTTPS to Firebase Authentication, which manages password hashing and verification. Weatherly does not keep a readable password in its profile database or local account files. It does not receive your Google or Facebook password.

Firebase retains session information on your device so you can remain signed in.

Location, town names and local preferences

With permission, Weatherly obtains individual device-location fixes for your forecast. Coordinates, reported accuracy, capture time and a town/area name may be cached on the device. Android’s system geocoder may resolve coordinates to a town using its provider’s network service.

Forecast requests send coordinates to Open-Meteo. The app does not intentionally include your Weatherly username, first name or Firebase user ID in those forecast requests. Refreshing weather can reuse the saved location; the crosshair requests a new fix.

The app may save local appearance/effects preferences and a temporary account identifier to resume an interrupted deletion. These are not extra fields in your cloud profile.

Technical information

Service providers may receive technical information such as IP addresses, request times and app/device information when their services are used. If you email us, we receive the message, sender address and information you choose to include.

SOS messages and nearby alerts

SOS setup stores the contact names, phone numbers, message, confirmed Android SMS subscription and account identifier on this device. The identifier prevents setup carrying over to another signed-in account. These contacts are not uploaded to Firebase or read from your full address book. SOS preferences are excluded from standard Android app backup and device-transfer rules.

Holding SOS for three seconds requests one location fix. With permission and an available configured SIM, Weatherly submits the message and available location details through your carrier to the contacts you selected. Carrier charges may apply. A sent status does not confirm recipient delivery or emergency-service dispatch.

Nearby sharing and receiving are optional. If sharing is enabled, participating nearby devices can read and relay your name, message, exact available coordinates, capture time and accuracy. An Android Keystore installation key protects message integrity but does not verify a person. Google Play Services Nearby provides offline connections and may process technical usage information under Google terms. Alerts are not uploaded to Firebase.

Receiving uses a visible foreground-service notification and can be stopped in SOS or from that notification. It does not continuously obtain location. Android settings, permissions, radio conditions and battery policies may interrupt reception. Reopen Weatherly after a reboot or force-stop to resume enabled receiving.

Payloads expire after at most 15 minutes. Up to ten received alerts and the last outgoing incident are kept locally while active; expired records are pruned when the service or SOS screen next processes them. Expiry limits display and forwarding, and cannot erase copies others have saved. SMS copies in your phone messaging app, on recipient phones and in carrier systems follow their own retention; clearing Weatherly does not erase those messages.

Clear SOS data in the SOS tab to remove local contacts, message, alerts and the signing key and stop receiving. Sign-out or completed in-app account deletion also invokes this cleanup on that phone. If interrupted, clear Android app storage. An email cloud-account deletion cannot remotely erase an offline phone or recall messages already shared.

How information is used

  • Verify accounts, maintain sessions and support account recovery.
  • Display your first name and retrieve weather for the selected coordinates.
  • Remember local preferences and resume interrupted operations.
  • Protect accounts, resolve service problems and handle privacy/deletion requests.

Location fixes are requested individually. The app does not continuously track location in the background.

Services involved

  • Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore: account authentication and the custom profile. Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Sign-In: provider verification. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Facebook Login: provider verification. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Open-Meteo: forecasts requested using coordinates. Open-Meteo terms and privacy information.
  • Android system geocoding: coordinate-to-town lookup; processing depends on the system provider.

These services may process information outside your country. Their own policies govern information they independently collect and retain.

Retention and account deletion

Your Weatherly account and custom profile are retained while the account is active, unless you request deletion or another applicable retention requirement applies.

The app’s deletion flow removes the custom Firestore profile and Firebase Authentication record. It may ask you to verify a linked provider again. An interrupted deletion can be retried; the app confirms completion after both removal steps finish.

The app attempts to clear its cached location on the current device after deletion. Other local preferences, recovery markers or cached files may remain if device cleanup fails; Android’s app-storage controls can remove remaining local data.

To request deletion without using the app, visit the Weatherly data-deletion page or email nexoraicc2026@gmail.com. Our processing target is within 30 days after verifying the account owner. We will explain if more information or additional time is needed.

Deleting Weatherly does not delete your Google or Facebook account or automatically erase independent provider logs/backups. Technical/security records may be retained under provider policies or applicable obligations. Request correspondence is retained only as needed to verify, handle and document the request, subject to applicable requirements.

Your controls and requests

You can manage location permission through Android settings, request a new fix with the crosshair, turn decorative weather effects off in Account and manage Weatherly access in your Google/Facebook account settings.

Contact nexoraicc2026@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of your Weatherly data. We may need to verify account ownership. Do not send passwords, login verification codes or access tokens.

Uninstalling the app, clearing device storage or revoking provider access does not itself delete the Weatherly cloud account.

Age requirement

Weatherly is intended for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 13, do not create an account. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact nexoraicc2026@gmail.com so we can investigate and arrange deletion where appropriate.

About these webpages

These static pages do not contain account sign-in forms or custom analytics. They use browser local storage to remember your chosen light/dark appearance. Email links open your email application; a request is submitted only when you send the message.

GitHub Pages hosts the published pages. GitHub may process visitor network/request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Security, updates and contact

Weatherly uses HTTPS for Firebase and forecast requests and uses Firebase authentication and access controls. No system can guarantee absolute security.

We may update this policy as the app or its information practices change. The effective date identifies this version.
