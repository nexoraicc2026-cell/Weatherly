# Privacy Policy

Weatherly respects your privacy. We collect only the information needed to provide the weather service and improve the experience.

## Information We May Collect

- Location data used to provide local weather updates
- Device and app usage information for troubleshooting and performance improvements
- Account information if you create or sign in to an account

## How We Use It

We use this information to:

- provide weather forecasts
- personalize the experience
- maintain security and service reliability
- comply with legal obligations

## Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share information only with trusted service providers that help operate the app or as required by law.

## Your Choices

You may choose not to share precise location data and may delete your account or related data through the settings or the data deletion page.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app owner through the support contact listed in the project repository.
