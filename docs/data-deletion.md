# Data Deletion

If you want to remove your personal data from Weatherly, please follow the steps below.

## In-App Options

- Open the app settings
- Select Account or Privacy settings
- Choose Delete Data or Remove Account

## Contact Option

If you do not see an in-app delete option, contact the project owner and request removal of your account and associated data.

## What May Be Deleted

Depending on your account and settings, we may remove:

- account profile information
- saved preferences
- location history
- usage information tied to your account

## Timeline

We aim to complete deletion requests as quickly as possible, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.
